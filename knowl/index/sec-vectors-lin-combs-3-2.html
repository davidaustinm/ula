A vector is most simply thought of as a matrix with a single column. For instance, \(\vvec = \left[ \begin{array}{r} 2 \\ 1 \\ \end{array} \right]\) and \(\wvec = \left[ \begin{array}{r} -3 \\ 1 \\ 0 \\ 2 \\ \end{array} \right]\) are both vectors. The entries in a vector are called its components. Since the vector \(\vvec\) has two components, we say that it is a two-dimensional vector; in the same way, the vector \(\wvec\) is a four-dimensional vector.
